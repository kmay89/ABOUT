# The stroganoff — the kitchen table's second recipe

Weeknight beef stroganoff: thin strips of sirloin seared hard, mushrooms
and onion browned in the same pan, a quick gravy from the brown bits,
and sour cream stirred in off the heat, over buttered egg noodles. About
forty minutes, one skillet and one pot.

## What's in it

- **The dial** weighs steak, from ½ lb to 6 lb, and the plate count and
  the pan follow: a 12-inch skillet up to 2 lb, a Dutch oven past that.
- **The vegetarian swap** puts thick-sliced portobellos where the steak
  was, pound for pound, with vegetable broth and vegetarian
  Worcestershire (the regular kind has anchovies). Salt waits until the
  mushrooms have browned.
- **The noodle step has the optional timer.** Everything else runs on
  how the pan looks, not on a clock.
- **The taste card** is written for a cream sauce: salt, Worcestershire
  and Dijon first; sour cream or butter to move the tang either way;
  broth to loosen; gentle heat, never a boil, to tighten.

## Checking it

At 1 lb in American measures the list should read: 1 lb steak, ½ lb
mushrooms, 1 small onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 Tbsp oil, 2 Tbsp butter,
¾ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, 1½ Tbsp flour, 1 cup broth, 1 Tbsp
Worcestershire, 1 tsp Dijon, ⅔ cup sour cream and ½ lb egg noodles,
feeding about 4 in a 12-inch skillet. Then walk the dial in both systems
and flip to vegetarian, as with the chili.
