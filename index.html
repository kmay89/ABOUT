The impossible object

This is a Mandelbulb — the three-dimensional cousin of the Mandelbrot set — and it is being computed right now, on your device, by one small program running on the GPU. No libraries, no model files: every pixel casts a ray and marches it toward a shape defined only by math. Drag to turn it. Scroll or pinch to lean in. Slide the power and watch infinity change its mind.
