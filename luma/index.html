What these two numbers do
  • Lightest areas is the thinnest plastic — where your photo glows brightest. 0.6–0.8 mm is the sweet spot. Thinner glows brighter but can print with gaps and glare; thicker makes highlights dimmer and greyer.
  • Darkest areas is the thickest plastic — where light is held back for shadows. About 3 mm suits white PLA. Thicker gives deeper shadows but a dimmer picture and a longer print; thinner is brighter but shadows wash out.
  • Match your light. A bright LED panel or light box can take more (3.2–3.6 mm). A tea light or dim nightlight looks better with less (2.2–2.6 mm).

Not sure? Print the test strip first. Seven steps from 0.6 to 3.6 mm, about 20 minutes. Hold it in front of the light you’ll really use, then tell Luma what you saw.
